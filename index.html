About

Operations taught me the questions. Analytics is teaching me the answers.

At LTIMindtree I was the person the escalation call routed to. Shift lead on a round-the-clock production support project — owning handovers, allocating incidents across the team on shift, and watching Control-M batch chains for the failure that would take a downstream business process with it.

What stayed with me was not the firefighting. It was noticing that the same tickets kept coming back, and that the SLA data already said why. Cutting resolution time by 30% came from reading that data, not from working faster.

I am at Great Lakes now to build the formal side of that instinct — statistics, modelling, and the business framing that turns an analysis into a decision someone will actually act on.
